Who We Are

Born from a deep love for authentic good food.

Founded in 2026, CraveBite began with a simple observation: while delicious food was being cooked in neighborhood kitchens and family bistros all around town, getting that food delivered steaming hot without high fees or long waits was a frustrating challenge.

We designed CraveBite to bridge that gap. By empowering restaurants with digital menu management and dispatching verified delivery riders equipped with GPS routing, we make gourmet cuisine, home-style rice & curry, woodfired pizza, and artisan desserts accessible with a couple of taps.

Whether you are ordering an instant meal for dinner tonight or scheduling a catering lunch for your team tomorrow, we treat every dish with care and urgency.
