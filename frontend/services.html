Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Scheduled Delivery work?

During checkout, select 'Schedule for Later' and choose your preferred date and time slot. The kitchen will prepare the dish right before the delivery window so it arrives hot.

Can I cancel an order once placed?

Yes! If the order is in the initial 'PLACED' status before the restaurant starts cooking, you can cancel it with one click from 'My Orders'.

What payment methods can I use?

We accept major Credit & Debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex) saved securely under your profile, as well as Cash on Delivery.
