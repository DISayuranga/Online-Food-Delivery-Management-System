Frequently Asked Questions

How fast will my food arrive?

Most deliveries arrive within 30 to 45 minutes of being placed, depending on dish preparation times and rider distances.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes! As long as the order is in the initial 'PLACED' status before the restaurant starts preparing it, you can cancel it with one click in 'My Orders'.

How can restaurants join CraveBite?

Restaurant owners can contact our team or be onboarded by the Administrator to manage their dishes, orders, and sales reports directly.

What payment methods are supported?

We accept major Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex) saved securely in your profile, as well as Cash on Delivery.
